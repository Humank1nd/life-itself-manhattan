## Source-informed starting point, with boundaries

- UW–Madison laboratory rat reference: **5–6 grams of food / 100 grams of body weight / day**. Source: https://www.rarc.wisc.edu/tools_and_guides/species_general_data.html
- National Academies' laboratory diet estimates use **15 g/rat/day** as an assumption for certain diet conversions, not as a universal per-rat feeding law. Source: https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/4758/chapter/4
- Rats' actual ingestion and hoarding are distinct outcomes that respond differently to body mass and foraging cost. Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2802007/

**Transfer warning:** These are laboratory measurements/assumptions, not direct calibrated wild-Manhattan estimates. Wet waste mass is not equivalent to dry laboratory feed mass or absorbed calories. Do not turn daily intake into a fixed single-bite limit. A bio-physical model must account for body weight, age, state, activity, food composition and time.
