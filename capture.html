Renderer-neutral capture — Times Square pilot

This tool renders the authoritative current building geometry (182 buildings within 320 m of the Times Square origin) from the shared three-waypoint camera path and captures three passes per waypoint: RGB, depth, and per-building segmentation, plus a run manifest with every camera pose in ENU meters and UE centimeters. The captures belong to no renderer; any model or evaluator can consume them.

Honesty rails: the geometry is the current snapshot only and makes no historical claim. The camera path is a synthetic demo path, not the 2005 photograph's camera. Captures are evaluation inputs, not evidence. Model training is not authorized.
